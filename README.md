# Walmart-3-DCF-model
What the Model Includes
Historical financial statement analysis
Bear, Base, and Bull case assumptions
5-year revenue and operating forecast
NOPAT and unlevered free cash flow calculation
WACC-based discounted cash flow valuation
Terminal value using the Gordon Growth Method
Implied share price and upside/downside analysis
Final investment recommendation
What I Found
Walmart generates strong revenue, but operates on thin margins, which is common for large retail companies.
The company’s valuation is highly sensitive to small changes in operating margin, CapEx, WACC, and terminal growth.
Under my current assumptions, the DCF produced implied share prices below the current market price.
Based on the model, Walmart appeared overvalued relative to my Base Case valuation.
The analysis showed that Walmart would need stronger margin expansion, lower capital spending, or higher long-term free cash flow growth to justify a higher valuation.
