# Walmart-3-DCF-model
Model Summary

The DCF model includes:

Historical financial statement analysis
Bear, Base, and Bull case assumptions
5-year revenue forecast
Gross profit, operating expense, EBIT, and NOPAT projections
Depreciation & amortization and CapEx assumptions
Unlevered free cash flow calculation
WACC-based discounted cash flow valuation
Terminal value using the Gordon Growth Method
Implied share price analysis
Upside/downside comparison to Walmart’s market price
Final investment recommendation

What I Found

Walmart generates very strong revenue, but operates on thin margins, which is typical for a large retail business.
The valuation was highly sensitive to small changes in operating margin, CapEx, WACC, and terminal growth.
Under my current assumptions, the DCF implied Walmart’s intrinsic value was below its market price.
Based on the Base Case valuation, the model suggested Walmart appeared overvalued.
The analysis showed that Walmart would need stronger margin expansion, lower capital spending, or higher long-term free cash flow growth to justify a higher valuation.
