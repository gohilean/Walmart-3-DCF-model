# Walmart-3-DCF-model
Model Summary

📊 What the Model Includes
Historical financial statement analysis
Bear, Base, and Bull case assumptions
5-year revenue and operating forecast
NOPAT and unlevered free cash flow calculation
WACC-based DCF valuation
Terminal value using the Gordon Growth Method
Implied share price analysis
Upside/downside comparison to Walmart’s market price
Final investment recommendation

🔍 What I Found
Walmart has very strong revenue, but its profit margins are thin because it operates in a highly competitive retail industry.
Small changes in margin, CapEx, WACC, and terminal growth had a large impact on the final valuation.
Under my current assumptions, the model produced implied share prices below Walmart’s current market price.
Based on the Base Case, the model suggested Walmart appeared overvalued.
The analysis showed that Walmart would need stronger margin expansion, lower capital spending, or higher long-term free cash flow growth to support a higher valuation.
